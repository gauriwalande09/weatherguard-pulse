# WeatherGuard AI product refinement

## Goal
Elevate the existing prototype into a final-round demonstration product without changing its scope or removing functionality.

## Approach
1. Preserve the deep navy, electric blue, teal, and white identity while improving hierarchy, spacing, typography, and contrast.
2. Recompose the first dashboard view around a five-second decision path: monitored data, anomaly, explanation, verdict, and action.
3. Make anomaly detection the strongest workflow, including clearer evidence, fault-versus-weather comparison, and a polished live test injection.
4. Refine sensor health, analytics, stations, navigation, responsive behavior, states, accessibility, and restrained micro-interactions.
5. Validate every route and key interaction on desktop and mobile.

## Technical details
- Keep the existing mock-data service boundary and live simulation.
- Reuse the current route structure and semantic design tokens.
- Avoid unsupported model-performance claims and new product scope.
